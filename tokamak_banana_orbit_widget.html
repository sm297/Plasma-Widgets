Orbit and display

Teaching model, not an orbit solver. Mirror points use B(θ)=B₀R₀/(R₀+r cos θ) and conservation of magnetic moment. Banana width uses the order-of-magnitude estimate Δb≈qρ/√ε (ρ at the outboard equator, ε=r/R₀). The toroidal precession is a rough drift scaling, not a self-consistent neoclassical calculation. The orbit shape and time dependence are schematic; gyromotion is omitted. Major radius is fixed at R₀=3 m; trail history is capped.
