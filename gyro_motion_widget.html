Controls the gyro-orbit radius; zero gives motion along B only.
Negative: against B · zero: circular orbit · positive: along B.
Uniform-field teaching model. The sliders set v⊥ and signed v∥ directly (in millions of metres per second). The helix radius is ρ = γmv⊥/(|q|B), and its advance per turn is v∥T. Both dimensions use the same spatial scale, held fixed as you adjust velocities and B unless the orbit would leave the frame. Switching species resets that reference scale. The 3D view shows three gyroperiods and repeats; playback speed is independent of the physical gyroperiod. No electric field, drift, collisions, or magnetic mirrors are included.
