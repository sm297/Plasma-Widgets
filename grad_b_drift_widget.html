Local guiding-centre model: B(x) ≈ B₀(1 + x/LB) along +z. The lines are straight (zero curvature), while ∇B points along +x. The drawn helix uses a constant gyrofrequency evaluated at the centre plus the calculated grad-B guiding-centre drift; it is not an exact Lorentz-force orbit. Best when ρ/LB ≪ 1.
